# LeadPipe: Turn Anonymous Visitors into Qualified Leads

## Transform Your Website Traffic into Revenue

LeadPipe is a powerful business intelligence platform that identifies anonymous website visitors and provides actionable insights to help your sales team prioritize the best opportunities.

## Our Platform: Current Capabilities

### 🧠 Visitor Intelligence & Journey Analysis
- **360° Visitor Profiles**: Comprehensive visitor profiles with contact details, professional information, and demographic data
- **Advanced Timeline**: Visualize complete buyer journeys with session-based activity grouping
- **Behavioral Analytics**: Automatically tag visitors based on engagement patterns
- **Multi-select Filtering**: Quickly find prospects using multiple simultaneous filters
- **Intelligent Search**: Powerful search with auto-complete and semantic understanding

### 📊 Advanced Analytics Dashboard
- **Real-time KPI Tracking**: Monitor key performance indicators with live updates
- **Conversion Metrics**: Track visitor-to-lead and lead-to-opportunity conversion rates
- **Traffic Source Analysis**: Identify which channels drive your highest-value traffic
- **Content Performance**: Measure engagement across your website content
- **Custom Date Ranges**: Analyze data over any time period with flexible date selection

### 🏢 Company Intelligence
- **Company Profiles**: Detailed firmographic data including size, revenue, and industry
- **Technology Stack Detection**: Identify what software and systems prospect companies use
- **Account-based Insights**: Group visitors by organization for cohesive account strategies
- **Engagement Scoring**: Assess overall company interest across multiple visitors

### 🔔 Engagement & Notification System
- **Real-time Alerts**: Immediate notifications for high-value prospect activities
- **Intent Scoring**: Automatic ranking of leads based on engagement and buying signals
- **Activity Feed**: Live stream of visitor interactions across your digital properties

### 🎨 User Experience
- **Modern UI**: Clean, intuitive interface optimized for efficiency
- **Dark/Light Mode**: Support for both dark and light themes
- **Responsive Design**: Fully functional on all device types and screen sizes
- **Performance Optimized**: Lightning-fast page loads and interactions
